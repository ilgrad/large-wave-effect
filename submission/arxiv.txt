ABSTRACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We study the large-wave effect on the ring of $N$ beads and for the discrete
Schrodinger equation on it. For the impulse Green's function
$A_N=\sup_t\max_j|u_j(t)|$ we prove $A_N=(1/\pi)\ln N+O(\ln\ln\ln N)$ for every $N$. A
palindromic argument shows that the first $\varphi(2N)/2$ frequencies $2\sin(\pi r/N)$
are $\mathbb{Q}$-independent, so by Bohr and Kronecker a $(1-o(1))$ fraction of the
ceiling $U_N=(1/2N)\sum\csc(\pi r/N)$ can be aligned. We prove $A_N=U_N$ if and only
if $N$ is prime or a power of two -- equivalently, every integer frequency relation
has coefficient-sum divisible by four -- via an explicit root-of-unity obstruction;
the orbit closure has dimension $\varphi(2N)/2$. The order law extends to the
Dirichlet segment and to every Cartesian product $C_N\,\square\,H$. For the discrete
Schrodinger propagator $e^{-itL_N}$, unitarity forbids amplification of a localized
state, while the $\ell^\infty\to\ell^\infty$ amplification is $B_N=\Theta(\sqrt N)$
with $\liminf B_N/\sqrt N\ge c_0/\sqrt2$. The ceiling $\sqrt N$ is reached, as a
supremum, exactly when the orbit closure contains a flat phase vector: this holds for
$N=p$ and $N=p^2$, so $\limsup B_N/\sqrt N=1$, and fails for every even $N\ge6$ (a
Golay-pair obstruction) and, by exact certificates, for $N=15,21,27$. On the dispersive
time scale $t=O(N)$ the constant splits by parity (numerically $c_0/\sqrt2$ for even
$N$, $\beta_{odd}=0.928\ldots$ for odd $N$). For the focusing discrete NLS the linear
large wave persists under weak nonlinearity, the modulational-instability band is read
off the $L_N$ spectrum, a single-site excitation cannot disperse above $\gamma P=4$,
and localized breathers exist, the site-centred one orbitally stable in the
anti-continuum regime. Proofs are kept apart from numerical evidence; scripts and an
exact GAP/PARI/Julia/FriCAS/Rocq layer reproduce all claims.
